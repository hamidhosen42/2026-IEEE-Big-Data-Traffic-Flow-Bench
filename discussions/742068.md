# Task 2: which released data may a forecast use?

https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/2026-ieee-big-data-traffic-flow-bench/discussion/742068

**Ankiy** (2026-09-19T13:51):

Hi organizers, a clarification on the data allowed as input for Task 2 (online queue forecasting). For a window with origin T on day D of the validation or private split:

May the forecast use released observations from before the 60-minute history, i.e. earlier on day D and on days before D, from mainline_states_masked and ramp_states of the same split? This is all data from before T.
ramp_states still contains values inside the 30-minute horizon (T+5 … T+30). We assume these must not be used. Is that right?
Mainline observations resume after the 60-minute buffer that follows each horizon. We assume these must not be used either. Is that right?
For Task 1 (offline reconstruction), we assume any released observation in the split may be used, including other days and times after the target cell. Is that right?
Thanks!

**Traffice_Flow_Bench_Organizer** (2026-09-19T17:46):

Thanks for asking. The answer is the same for every team, so here it is on the record.

Task 2 is an online task. For a window with forecast origin T, a submission may use released data with a timestamp at or before T, and nothing after it.

Everything you listed as being before T is allowed: earlier times on day D, earlier days in the same split, and the training split in full. Any released file counts, including mainline_states_masked and ramp_states. Use as much of it as you want.

No data carrying a timestamp after T may be used, in any file. Both of your assumptions are correct. The values that sit inside the horizon in ramp_states are after T and are out of scope. Mainline observations that resume after the buffer are after T and are out of scope as well. The buffer is there so that a forecast cannot read the horizon directly. It is not a boundary that makes what comes after it usable.

For Task 1 your assumption is correct as well. Task 1 is offline. You see the whole evaluation period, and you may use any released observation in the split, at any time, including times after the target cell.

We will add both of these to the Rules page so they are binding rather than just a forum answer. Thanks for raising it, this needed to be stated precisely.
