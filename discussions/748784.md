Three observations from the current release, then the question.


The window IoU cannot be reproduced on any split. score_task2.py needs a queue_targets.parquet that is not in the release. Measured on the current data: the horizon cells of every Task 2 window have finite_frac = 0.0 in mainline_states_masked across all 8 panels on both validation and private (0.578 on train). So the only truth that can be reconstructed is on the train split, as speed <= v_cut on score-eligible cells. This looks like the fix for the issue reported in the "future cells are present in the released mainline_states_masked files" thread - those numbers no longer reproduce after the rebuild.


The train ruler is a single month. All 80 official train windows (8 panels x 10) fall in 2030-06. A model that was partly fit on that month reads optimistically there, and there is no month-to-month spread to measure stability against.


Cell-level accuracy on the deployment months does not predict the window metric. The masked layer does keep real observations on cells outside the windows, and those work well as a ruler for Task 1. For Task 2 they are not at the horizon timestamps, and in our runs gains measured on cells did not carry over to the six-step window IoU.

**Traffice_Flow_Bench_Organizer** (2026-10-15T04:10):

Thanks for the careful write-up. A few facts that may help, without commenting on how local numbers relate to the leaderboard.

The horizon is blank on validation and private by design, and the queue targets are withheld there, so the window IoU can only be reproduced on train. On train you can derive labels from train/mainline_states the way you describe. The official label is computed from the underlying state rather than the released observation, so observation noise near the threshold will cause small differences against your reconstruction.

You are right that the 80 official train windows all fall in June 2030. That comes from the same selection behavior described in the onset announcement: the selector keeps the earliest qualifying origins. Building windows across all nine train months with the published rule, as you did, is a sensible way to get a wider ruler.

Beyond that we don’t recommend a particular protocol, since finding one that transfers is part of the task. Sharing your harness publicly is welcome, and anything shared on the forum is available to everyone under the Rules.
