Everything the leaderboard scores has been regenerated. Please download the data again and submit again: scores from before today were computed against values that no longer exist.
- Why we rebuilt rather than patched

The first two problems were repaired in place, by removing the cells that gave the answer away. That is not enough on its own. Anyone who downloaded the package before those repairs still holds the old values, and blanking a cell afterwards does not take back a copy someone already has.

So the scenario behind the validation and private months was generated again from new random seeds. Every speed, every flow, every queue label in those two months is a different number now. A copy of the old release is not just useless against the new one, it is worse than useless: we measured it, and using it to answer the new Task 2 scores below a persistence baseline on all but one corridor.

The training month is unchanged. Its traffic is identical, cell for cell.

- What this means for you

Download the data again. All of it, not only the parts that changed last time.

Your code does not need changes. The four tasks, the scoring formula, the weights, the submission format and every column name are the same. The submission_id values are new, so take them from the new sample_submission.csv rather than from a saved copy.

Retrain and resubmit. Models fitted to the old months will not transfer. For most people this is one pass over the pipeline you already have.

Earlier scores stay on the leaderboard and we cannot remove them. We looked for a way to clear it and found none available to us as hosts. If you know of one, please leave a comment, we would be grateful. So for a while the leaderboard will show two kinds of score side by side: ones from the old release and ones from this one. A team ranked above you may simply not have resubmitted yet. Only submissions made after today count for awards, and we determine the final standings from those, using the submission timestamps Kaggle records. If you submitted before today, your position on the board means nothing until you resubmit.

- What else changed

While rebuilding we went through every file in the release rather than only the places we already knew about, and fixed three smaller things. The ramp files carried a speed column that was derived from the freeway rather than measured on the ramp; it is gone, with four location fields that were never real positions. Task 4 was being scored against the wrong month's weak prior on the public leaderboard, so Task 4 scores will read higher than before for the same method. An empty submission used to collect a small positive score and now scores zero.

- The baselines

We reran the same three baselines on the rebuilt release, and the organizer baseline is back on the leaderboard as the benchmark row.

- Thank you

To the participants who reported the first two problems: thank you. Both were found by people who looked carefully at the data and told us. If you find something else, please post it.

We are sorry for the disruption. This is the second time we have asked you to redo work, and we know some of you had put real effort into the months we have just replaced. Rebuilding was the only way we could see to make this fair to everyone, including people who never held the earlier release. Thank you for staying with it.
