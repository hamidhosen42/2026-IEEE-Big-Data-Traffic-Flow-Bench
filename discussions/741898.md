**chensiliang12** (2026-09-18T07:07):

Hi organizers, could you please clarify the eligibility criteria for the Student / Newcomer Award?
If this is my first Kaggle competition and I am currently a student, would I be eligible for this award?

---

**Traffice_Flow_Bench_Organizer** (2026-09-19T17:45):

Thanks for asking.

The Student / Newcomer Award has a single criterion: every member of the team must be an enrolled high school or undergraduate student on 6 November 2026. Graduate students are not eligible. That criterion is our definition of newcomer.

Kaggle account age and the number of competitions you have entered are not part of it. A new account does not make anyone a newcomer, and a long-standing account does not disqualify an undergraduate. We wanted a criterion that cannot be reset.

The award goes to the highest-placed team on the private leaderboard that meets this criterion and has not already received the Gold, Silver, or Bronze award. Eligibility is checked only for the team in line to receive it, at the same time as the code and reproducibility check described in the Rules, and we will ask each member for enrollment documentation at that point. If that team does not qualify, the award passes to the next team that does. If no team qualifies, the award is not given.

To your question: if you are currently a high school or undergraduate student, you are eligible, and whether this is your first Kaggle competition makes no difference. If you are a graduate student, you are not eligible.
