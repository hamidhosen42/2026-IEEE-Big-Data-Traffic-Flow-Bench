**Traffice_Flow_Bench_Organizer** (2026-10-15T04:14):

Thanks for the clear questions.


Yes, that is expected. mainline_states_masked has the Task 1 mask applied on top of detector availability, which removes 20, 30 or 50 percent of eligible cells depending on the regime. The 60-minute history spans of the Task 2 windows are exempt from the Task 1 mask, which is why window_history has higher coverage than an ordinary calendar span.

We don’t prescribe a validation protocol. Building train-only windows with the published selection rule is a reasonable approach. Train also ships with unmasked mainline_states, so you can choose spans or apply your own masking to reach the history coverage you want, without combining masking regimes or touching withheld labels.

Yes. For the input distribution of the scored windows, treat window_history as the reference, and expect calendar-derived proxy windows to be sampled differently.
