**Traffice_Flow_Bench_Organizer** (2026-10-15T04:13):

Thanks for asking before relying on it. Both readings are right, with one condition: everything used for a window, including inputs, model fitting, and model or threshold selection, must be stamped at or before that window’s T.

Yes. A model that forecasts a window with origin T may be refitted on released data stamped before T, including earlier days of the same split, such as training windows cut from that split’s mainline_states_masked. What is not allowed is a model fitted on a whole split and then applied to that split’s earlier windows, because it has used data stamped after those windows’ T.
Yes. For an April window, every released March file and the full training split are stamped before T, and may be used both as inputs and to fit or select the model. The reverse is forbidden, as you say: a March window may not use anything from the April files.

Declaring this in your report is appreciated.
