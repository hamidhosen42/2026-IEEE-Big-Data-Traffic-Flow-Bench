PRE-REGISTERED DECISION RULE -- EXP_T2_PHASE (half-hour clock phase features), written before any phase column, model or number.
Motivation (train truth only, caller's diagnostic): corridor onsets / sudden clearances peak at minute :05 and :35; official onset
origins cluster at :05 in train / validation / private.  'tod' is dropped by the np and id ongoing members (PRIOR_COLS / ID_DROP).
Compliance: the phase is a function of the clock time of T (and of the step h), known at T; no data after T is used.

PHASE COLUMNS (4; t = slot of T, 0..287, 5 min, slot 0 = 00:00; h = step 1..6, target slot t+h; slot s is a ':05/:35 slot' iff s mod 6 == 1)
  ph6     = t mod 6                                   (1 <=> T itself is at :05 / :35)
  ph_on   = 1[(t + h) mod 6 == 1]                     (step h lands on a :05/:35 slot)
  ph_next = 5 * (((-t) mod 6) + 1)                    (minutes from T to the next :05/:35 slot strictly after T: 5..30)
  ph_x    = 1[h * 5 >= ph_next]                       (step h is at or after that boundary = interval (T, T+h] crosses it)
