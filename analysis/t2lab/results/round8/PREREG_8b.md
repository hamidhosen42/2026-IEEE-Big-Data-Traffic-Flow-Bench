## Protocol (EXP_T2_OSP_MT, analysis/t2lab/exp_onset_marchtrain.py; production pv recipe)
pv model = CatBoost (600 it, depth 8, lr 0.08) trained on the train onset rows + the released-March onset windows with day < cut
(production onset rule, 72-slot spacing, observed labels), sample weight w for the March rows, one model per w in (10, 20, 40);
probability = mean over the 3 weights; features with the same-scenario prior blended (k = 4, scenario days strictly before the
window's day), production decoder. Evaluation = March onset windows with day ≥ cut, scored with observed labels as in OSP_MT.
- Cut 15 is the binding split (about 131 windows); cut 20 (about 88 windows, nested in cut 15) is a sign check.
- Single seed s = all 3 CatBoosts with random_seed s. R0 = mean IoU over the single seeds of the replicate; production = seed 0.
- Bag = for every w, the mean over seeds of the replicate (30 models), then the mean over the 3 weights.
- Replicates: seeds 0–9 (A) and 10–19 (B). Window-bootstrap 95 % CI, 2,000 draws within panel; non-tie panel = |Δ| > 1e-9.

## Gate (PASS-April-A) — all must hold
1. Cut 15 pooled Δ(bag − R0) ≥ +0.008 with CI lower bound > 0.
2. Each replicate (A, B) at cut 15 ≥ +0.004.
3. Cut 15: ≥ 70 % of non-tie panels positive and none below −0.02.
4. Cut 20 pooled Δ ≥ 0.
5. Reported, not gated: Δ against seed 0 (the current production pv), and bag-of-10 vs bag-of-20.

## Deployment rule

everything else of tfb-k9 unchanged; pick B (tfb-k10) unchanged. On FAIL: no change to either pick. No April labels, no public LB.
