CONVENTIONS
- lb_delta = public LB of the probe minus LB of its actual base (base_slug). Bases from kaggle_jobs/auto_chain_*.log and
  *_state.json: chain oct4b bases are sequential (c20 -> E1 -> E2 -> E3 -> E4 -> E5); chain oct5 (F1..F5) all vs E5;
  chain oct6 started from E5 (not F5): G1 vs E5, G2 vs G1, G3 vs G1 (so G3 = allfin + occ vs fs4), G4 vs G3, G5 vs G4;
  chain oct7: H1 vs G4, H2 built with W 0.5 (listed vs G4 = +0.00028; chain logged -0.00009 vs H1), H3/H4/H5 vs H1;
  oct3: c16 vs COMBO15, c17 vs c16, c18 vs c17, c19 vs c18, c20 vs c18. COMBO2D/COMBO3/COMBO4/COMBO5 are one-factor
  siblings (research/86217_vs_85860_diff.md); COMBO15 is on COMBO13 (not COMBO14).
- lb_iou_delta = lb_delta/0.15 for onset or ongoing rows; = lb_delta/0.30 for both-T2 rows (mean IoU of both conditions).
- Lab cells: IoU deltas (new arm - base arm) of the condition changed. official40 = official train windows (40 per condition,
  fold 0 / 4-fold); offlike = official-like train OOF (ongoing 2,992 windows; onset fold-0 n=522); pseudo = current-style
  pseudo windows in train (4-fold); offlike_m / runstart_m = pseudo-March ongoing sets (298 / 1124 windows);
  gate_other = other pseudo-March numbers (EXP_024 gate, pmarch2, v16/v17 gate, pseudo-March 214 onset windows,
  month-start replay). For the both-T2 rows the lab cell is S_queue (both conditions).
- lab_T1_proxy is on the LB-point scale: either 0.35 x val-month-holdout dS_state (rows 07,08,14,20,22,23,26,28,17,19), or a
  lab J = 0.35 dS_state + 0.10 dLWR + 0.05 dS_FD (rows 34,37,38,53,54,57), or 0.35 x blackout-row dS_state (row 48).
  ratio_T1 = lb_delta / lab_T1_proxy. For holdout rows LB/dS_state = ratio_T1 x 0.35 (≈0.85-0.92 for D/E/F/FT).
- Ratio = LB IoU delta / lab IoU delta. A negative ratio means the signs disagree.
- PRE/POST: each sources entry is tagged. PRE = recorded before the probe. POST = recorded after it, or computed on 10-08 by a
  search agent from caches (exact arm, or the closest analog). When the exact arm was only available post-hoc, the cell
  holds the exact-arm value and the PRE proxy goes in the note (e.g. ON30).
- Row 24 (COMBO7) also retrained the CatBoost onset, so it is not a clean one-factor probe. Row 07 (SUB_A) also changed T4.
- Rows with NO lab number (kept for completeness): 29 COMBO12 bag-2, 32 COMBO15 bag-4, 39 E2 og8, 43 F1. Rows 35 og4 and
  33 bag-8 have only an LB forecast. Rows 51/52/55/58 are private-only (public unchanged by construction).
- Excluded probes: #1-#5 (copy of a public notebook, two upload errors, the base public notebook); no lab.
