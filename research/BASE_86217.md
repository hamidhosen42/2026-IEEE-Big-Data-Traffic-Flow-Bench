## Task 1 — `tfb-t1v7-full`, variant E
- **Grid:** per panel, a (day, 288, station) cube, with stations sorted by milepost. This is the same order the Task 3 scorer uses (topology audit below).
- **Base:** per-station temporal linear interpolation of speed and per-lane flow. LightGBM predicts the residual of the normalised values: v/v_f and q/lane.
- **Features:**
  - temporal and spatial interpolations, including occupancy;
  - neighbours at ±1 and ±2 stations for t−1, t and t+1;
  - the station's own values at t±1 and t±2;
  - occupancy neighbours;
  - gap distances;
  - ratio transfer from the ±1 neighbours;
  - lanes, v_f, position, regime rate;
  - rank-3 and rank-5 per-day SVD of the interpolated fields, occupancy low-rank, and cross-day per-station low-rank.
  - There are **no** weekday × time profile features (NOPROF).
- **Training:**
  - rows are the official train template cells;
  - training runs on a "darkened" view with 90-min blackouts after the replicated Task 2 origins;
  - up to 2 × 600k rows are sampled.
- **Model E:** 255 leaves, lr 0.05, min_data 100, max_bin 63, feature/bagging fraction 0.8, λ_L2 = 1, 900 trees, speed and flow models trained separately.
- **Post-processing:**
  - speed clipped to [3, 1.05·v_f];
  - flow = max(…, 20).
- **Validation:** val-month internal holdout (15% of released eligible cells, seed 7) = **0.9374**.

## Task 2 — `tfb-t2v12`, variant big255
- **Links:** sorted by `order_index`, and all 8 panels are pooled into one model.
  - The topology audit (2026-10-01) shows the travel direction is **reversed** in the W/S panels relative to the E/N panels.
- **History:** [T−60, T), plus the masked slot-T value.
- **Features:**
  - speed / v_cut at lags, min, mean and slope; flow;
  - neighbours ±1..3;
  - band minima over links +1..3, +4..8 and +9..16 on each side (named `dn` / `up` by array direction);
  - distance to the nearest queued link now, 15 min ago and 30 min ago;
  - queue share;
  - pos, lanes, cap_lane, capacity ratios, length, tod, dow;
  - weekday/weekend-class priors: `prior`, `prior_now`, `prior_alldays`.
- **Onset** (`queue_onset`):
  - CatBoost with depth 8, 600 iterations, lr 0.08, trained on up to 1.2M rows with all features;
  - it predicts **T+30 only**, and T+5..T+25 are empty.
- **Ongoing** (`queue_ongoing`):
  - LightGBM with 255 leaves, min_data 50, lr 0.05, 1000 trees;
  - it is **prior-free**: it drops prior, prior_now, prior_alldays, pos, tod, dow, lanes, cap_lane, length and cap_ratio_hi/lo;
  - training cells are the "broad keep" region: within 12 links of a queue now, 15 min ago or 30 min ago, or slow, or prior ≥ 0.05.
- **Training windows:** the replicated official selector over the 9 train months, with dense ongoing sampling (MAX_OG = 1500 per panel).
- **Decoder:** expected-IoU top-m, `argmax_m Σtop p / (m + Σrest p)`, applied per window.
- **Candidate radius:** there is no hard radius at inference. The keep region only restricts ongoing *training* cells. At inference every link is scored and the decoder chooses m.
