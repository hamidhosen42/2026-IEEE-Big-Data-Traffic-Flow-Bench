**Key facts about the data (verified):**
- The data is synthetic, generated by a calibrated traffic model (CTM/LWR-like) with measurement noise and incidents.
  - Validation has 5 incidents; private has 7.
  - Validation has more weekend windows (35% vs 24% in train) and more non-recurrent queues (discussion + a public repo).
- The Task 2 window selector is replicated exactly for onset: the greedy chronological scan with 360-min global spacing matches 40/40 official train onset origins.
  - In the onset truth, the queue appears only at T+30 (organizer confirmed 120/120).
  - Onset windows: no queue visible in history [T-60, T). Ongoing: at least 2 queued observations in history with at least 2 on one link. Persistence IoU ≤ 0.9. Coverage ≥ 0.7.
- The Task 2 truth comes from the noise-free underlying state; our training labels are noisy observations.
  - The CV→LB ratio is ≈0.84 for our model and ≈0.82 for official persistence, so the gap is systematic.
- The Task 3 LWR score uses organizer boundary flows projected onto the released observations. Local proxy: `1 − Σ|ΔN_pred − ΔN_obs|/Σ|ΔN_obs|`, where N = q/v·L on target cells and non-target cells use released truth. So LWR ≈ an L1 accuracy of the implied density at masked cells.
- Task 1 masks are i.i.d. per (day, time, link) by hash at 20/30/50%.
  - Val/private also blank a 90-min window (T+5..T+90) after every Task 2 origin, and Task 1 targets inside that window make up about 1.9% of targets.
- Link order is milepost = order_index for all Task 2 panels.
- Task 4 counts are noise-free. NNLS λ=0.05 on the split's own counts and prior gives S_ODME ≈ 0.994 (public probes).

## Task 1 (state) — current: LightGBM residual to per-station temporal interpolation
- **Features:**
  - temporal and spatial (milepost, total flow) interpolation;
  - neighbours ±1,±2 stations at t−1, t, t+1; self t±1, t±2;
  - occupancy neighbours plus temporal/spatial interpolation;
  - gap distances, ratio transfer, lanes, v_free, position, regime rate;
  - per-day rank-3 and rank-5 SVD of the interpolated fields, occupancy low-rank, cross-day per-station SVD.
- **No** weekday×time-of-day profile features (profile-free transfers better to the new month).
- Trained with simulated 90-min blackouts after Task 2 origins; speed capped at 1.05·v_free.
- Model: 255 leaves, lr 0.05, 900 trees, up to 3M rows.
- Validation: validation-month internal holdout (15% of released eligible cells hidden) = 0.9374.

## Task 2 (queue) — current: per-condition cell classifiers + expected-IoU top-m decoding
- **Onset:** CatBoost (depth 8, 600 iterations) on all features, h = T+30 only.
- **Ongoing:** prior-free LightGBM (no location/time/prior features), 255 leaves, 1000 trees. Cells are kept near queue activity: within 12 links of a queue now / 15 / 30 min ago, or slow, or prior ≥ 0.05 (coverage ≈ 99.6%).
- **Features, all computed on data before T:**
  - speed/v_cut at lags, min, mean, slope; flow/capacity; last-valid ffill; the masked slot-T value;
  - neighbours ±1..3; band minima up/downstream;
  - distance to the nearest queued link now and 15/30 min ago; corridor queue share;
  - lanes, capacity ratios, position, tod, dow;
  - queue prior P(queued | link, slot, weekday/weekend class).
- **Training windows:** replicated selector over 9 train months with dense ongoing sampling (≤ 3000 per panel).

## Findings on 2026-10-01 (see `research/SCORE_GAP_ANALYSIS.md` and `research/topology_audit.md`)
- **Task 2 direction bug.** Links are sorted by order_index, but travel runs toward increasing index in the E/N panels and decreasing index in the W/S panels. The pooled model's up/dn features are therefore flipped in 4 of 8 panels. The fix was tested (EXP_021) and hurt: pseudo −0.003. DROP, because the model compensates.
- **Task 3 is pinned by noise.** S_LWR ≈ 0.52 for interpolation and for the model alike. Realistic upside is ≤ +0.003 total.
- **The frontier gap is Task 2.** A 0.90 score needs S_queue ≈ 0.89; ours is ≈ 0.78.
